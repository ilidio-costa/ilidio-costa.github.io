**Organization:** Addcreative | **Role:** Material Engineer Intern | **Timeline:** February 2023 - October 2023

## 1. Introdution

During my internship at Adira Addcreative, a specialist in large-scale metal additive manufacturing recently acquired by SLM Solutions. I conducted research for my Bachelor's dissertation focused on the industrial application of Laser Powder Bed Fusion (L-PBF). Working with the revolutionary Addcreator machine, which utilizes "Tiled Laser Melting" (TLM) technology, I focused on bridging the gap between raw feedstock properties and final print quality for large-scale engineering components.

## 2. Technical Experience

- <b>Comprehensive Feedstock Characterization:</b> Conducted detailed analysis of metal powder feedstocks, evaluating critical parameters including apparent and tapped density, flowability, morphology, and particle size distribution.

- <b>Powder Degradation Study:</b> Investigated the evolution of metal powders over successive print cycles and recirculations within the Addcreator system to determine the impact of reuse on material integrity.

- <b>Industrial L-PBF Operation:</b> Gained hands-on experience with cutting-edge, large-format L-PBF technology, understanding the complexities of managing closed metal powder circulation systems.

- <b>Standardized Testing:</b> Applied rigorous materials science protocols to ensure that feedstock met the high-performance requirements of sectors such as aerospace and energy.

## 3. Take-aways

- <b>Metallurgical Precision in AM:</b> Developed a deep understanding of how physical powder specifications directly influence the laser-material interaction and final part density in metal 3D printing.

- <b>Resource Efficiency & Sustainability:</b> Learned to evaluate and manage the life cycle of expensive metal alloys, optimizing powder reuse strategies to reduce waste in industrial manufacturing.

- <b>Technical Problem Solving:</b> Gained the ability to identify critical failure points in the additive manufacturing workflow, such as powder contamination, and implement engineering solutions like integrated sieving.

- <b>R&D Documentation:</b> Refined the ability to conduct independent scientific research, translating experimental data into a formal dissertation that provides actionable insights for industrial AM processes.
